About

An operator's view of restaurant software

I've spent four years on restaurant and event floors as a server, bartender, event captain and manager, most recently running daily operations for a three-floor restaurant in Rockefeller Center. That taught me where restaurant software helps and where it quietly creates work: systems that don't talk to each other, spreadsheets someone keeps by hand, managers stuck at a laptop instead of on the floor.

So I built the fix myself, got it running, and trained the team on it. That's the work I want to do full time: implementation, onboarding and customer success for hospitality tech. Getting restaurants configured, trained and live, in the language operators actually speak.

Took Nightly Close from a messy manual process to a tool managers used every night: mapped the workflow, built it, handled the edge cases (role changes, lunch vs. dinner pools), and trained the managers. Earlier, set up service flow and closing procedures on a restaurant opening team.

Featured project · 2026

Nightly Close

Every night, a manager had to pull exports from three systems and hand-key tips into a 16-tab Excel workbook before payroll. I built a web app that reads those exports, applies the tip-pool rules, checks the punches, and hands back payroll-ready entries plus a short list of the calls a manager actually has to make. Then I rolled it out and trained the managers who used it.
